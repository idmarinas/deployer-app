When you delete an item from the interface, :vars{n="project"} **does not remove the row from the database**: it sets its deletion date (`deleted_at`). This behavior is shared by **hosts, commands, access keys and variables**.

## Where deleted items go

A deleted item no longer appears in normal queries and is shown in the module's **Deleted** tab and in the **Trash** page in the sidebar, where items are grouped by module.

The deletion notification offers **Undo** for a few seconds. You can also restore the item from the trash or from the **Deleted** tab.

## Restore and permanent deletion

**Restore** returns the item to the **Active** or **Inactive** tabs according to its state. **Permanent deletion** removes it from the database, asks for confirmation and cannot be undone; it can be done per item or by emptying a module's trash, with double confirmation.

::warning
When emptying the trash, rows with references from other modules or **internal** rows —such as the seed commands used by the application— are left in the trash. The application reports how many rows were blocked.
::

If the dialog detects references when deleting or purging an item, it lists them and offers **Unlink and delete**: before continuing, those references are removed. For variables, uses in commands are listed as a warning and the dialog offers **Delete anyway**, because those expressions will stop resolving when the commands run.

::note
Unique names and keys remain occupied while the item is in the trash: uniqueness validations also count those rows. To reuse a name, slug or key, restore the item or permanently delete it.
::
